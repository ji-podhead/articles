Glossary: market segments and customer types

Who buys and runs this stuff - from the teams this guide targets to the partners who run it for them.
SMB / SME
Small and medium-sized businesses (EN) / KMU (DE) - teams usually without a dedicated security function; the target market of this guide.
KMU
German abbreviation for SMB (kleine und mittlere Unternehmen).
MSP
Managed Services Provider - runs IT, and often security, for SMBs.
MSSP
Managed Security Services Provider - the security-specialized MSP: 24/7 detection and response for many clients at once.
SaaS
Software as a Service - cloud software delivered over the internet; a SOC 2 report is usually a purchasing prerequisite here.
Enterprise
Large organizations: multi-account and multi-cloud governance, central policies, compliance programs - a different sales motion than SMB.
