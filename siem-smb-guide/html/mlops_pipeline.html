Anomaly detection as a managed lifecycle, not a one-off notebook

The false-positive gate and the retrain loop are what separate a production detector from a script that ran once.
TRAIN - AND GATE
1 · Features
gateway / app logs: bytes_sent rolling windows, unique_dest_ips - the same egress numbers the rules see.
→
2 · Training
Isolation Forest / Random Cut Forest - SageMaker Pipelines or MLflow track the run.
→
3 · Evaluation gate
false-positive rate below 1% or it does not ship - an alert-spam generator is worse than no detector.
↓ approved by a human
SERVE - AND WATCH
4 · Model registry
versioned, pending manual approval - the model in production is always a choice, never a surprise.
→
5 · Realtime endpoint
scores every event: {anomaly_score, confidence, model_version}.
→
6 · Model monitor
live traffic vs. the training distribution - drift fires an alarm, not a shrug.
6 → 1 · continuous retrain - legitimate behavior changes must not break the model
The golden rule
An ML-generated finding carries confidence + model_version and adds to the rule layer - it never replaces it. A detector that cannot explain why it fired is an alert-spam generator with extra steps.
