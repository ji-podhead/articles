Glossary: frameworks and certifications

The compliance anchors a SIEM's retention and reporting layers have to satisfy.
SOC 2
Audit standard for SaaS operations (security, availability, confidentiality) - proof-of-controls report by an independent CPA firm.
HIPAA
US law protecting health data (PHI); mandatory for healthcare workloads.
PCI-DSS
Payment card industry security standard - mandatory for anything touching card data, including your billing flows.
NIST CSF
US framework catalog for security programs; the "N" many evidence-pack exports are mapped to.
CIS Benchmarks
Center for Internet Security configuration catalogs (e.g. the AWS Foundations Benchmark) that posture tools score your account against.
GDPR / DSGVO
EU data protection law - the reason security tooling must prove what it stores, not just that it stores it.
