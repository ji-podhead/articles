Your infrastructure is indexed before you finish deploying it. Not by a human — by search engines that continuously scan the entire internet, grab every banner, and make it searchable in under a second.

Shodan, Censys, FOFA, ZoomEye, Quake, Grayhat Warfare, LeakIX — none of them exploit anything. They just automate what you could do yourself with curl and a lot of patience: mass port scanning, banner grabbing, indexing. That's how an exposed Ollama instance, an open S3 bucket, or an unauthenticated vector database gets found — often before its owner knows it's reachable at all.

Part 1 catalogs the actual tools and the actual queries: ASN-based hosting-provider filtering, CVE-direct search, JARM fingerprinting for spoofed banners, and where secrets leak from public code. Part 2 (next) mirrors every technique into the fix.
