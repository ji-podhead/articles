Fuzzy sets: membership degrees for a temperature controller

Classical logic knows two states. Fuzzy logic (Zadeh, 1965) knows degrees of truth: membership grades between 0 and 1, so one reading carries two partial memberships at once.
cold
1.0 up to 12 °C
falls linearly to 0 at 22 °C
warm
0 at 15 °C
1.0 from 22 to 28 °C
0 again at 35 °C
hot
0 up to 30 °C
1.0 from 38 °C
Membership functions μ(T) - one 19 °C reading, two memberships: 0.30 cold and 0.57 warm
The rule fires with a degree
IF temperature IS warm AND rising, THEN reduce heating slightly - at 19 °C it fires at degree 0.57. No cliff, no hard threshold: rules blend continuously.
Why it mattered then - and now
Fuzzy logic let early AI reason over vague, rule-encoded knowledge. Its descendants are everywhere in modern RAG: relevance scores and membership-style gates on what enters memory are degrees of truth in new clothes.
