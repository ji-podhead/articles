In the 1980s, expert systems ran on Lisp machines the size of a fridge. Today the same ideas run behind an API.

Knowledge base + inference machine. Rules with degrees of truth. Agents that remember, reason and act. Forty years of knowledge-based AI — and the current agent wave works best when it re-imports what that era got right: explicit knowledge, explicit rules, explicit verification.

Hallucination is not a new problem. It is the oldest one, wearing a new interface.
