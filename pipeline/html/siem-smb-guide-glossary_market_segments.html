Glossary: Market Segments & Customer Types

TERM
MEANING
SMB / SME
Small and Medium-sized Businesses (EN) / Kleine und mittlere Unternehmen - KMU (DE); firms up to ~500 employees, usually without a dedicated security team. The target market for "conveyor-belt" security products.
MSP
Managed Services Provider - runs IT (and often security) for SMBs.
MSSP
Managed Security Services Provider - the security-specialized MSP; runs 24/7 detection/response for many clients.
SaaS
Software as a Service - cloud software delivered over the internet; SOC 2 reports are usually a purchasing prerequisite for SaaS buyers.
Enterprise
Large organizations: multi-account/multi-cloud governance, central policies, compliance programs - a different sales motion than SMB.
KMU
German abbreviation for SMB (kleine und mittlere Unternehmen).
