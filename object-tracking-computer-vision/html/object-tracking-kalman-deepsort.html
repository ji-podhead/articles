Object Trackers: Kalman Filter + DeepSORT

Keeping identity alive through occlusion, motion blur, and objects that leave the frame
Kalman Filter
One of the most common and most powerful tracking techniques, still used today because it is very fast, and often combined with neural-network algorithms.
STEP 1 · PREDICTION
From the object's past known states (position, velocity), predict where it should be in the next moment.
STEP 2 · CORRECTION
When a new measurement arrives, compare it with the prediction and correct, weighting prediction vs. measurement by their respective uncertainties.
DeepSORT
Deep Learning-based Simple Online and Realtime Tracking: deep learning added to SORT for robust multi-object tracking.
TWO MODULES, FUSED
Motion: a Kalman filter predicts trajectories. Appearance: deep re-identification (ReID) holds identity across frames. Data association aligns detections with existing tracks.
KEY ADVANTAGES
Robust to occlusions, motion blur, and appearance changes; high accuracy from the motion + appearance synergy; efficient across many targets; suited to dynamic settings like gesture recognition.
MOTA · Multi-Object Tracking Accuracy
MOTA = 1 - (FP + FN + IDS) / GT
FP: false positives (wrong detections) · FN: false negatives (missed objects) · IDS: identity switches (wrong ID assignment) · GT: ground-truth occurrences. Higher is better, 100% = perfect. MOTP covers position precision.
Tools & benchmarks
py-motmetrics (Python MOT evaluation) · MOT Challenge (official benchmark results) · OpenCV (all tracker models available, combinable with YOLO detections) · OC-SORT + CMC (refined motion modeling).
