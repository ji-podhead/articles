Structuring · Normalization · Harmonization

Three distinct disciplines of a production data pipeline · mixing them up breaks schemas and skews models
1 · Structuring
Raw, unorganized facts go into a defined, predictable format: tables, JSON files, or graphs.
WHAT GOES IN
Unstructured data follows no predefined format, lives in data lakes, and needs data-science work to read.
WHAT COMES OUT
Structured data: predefined categories and fields, like a contact form's name, phone, and email.
WHY IT MATTERS
Turns unorganized text and streams into machine-readable, queryable formats.
→
2 · Normalization
Cleaning and reorganizing the structured data to reduce duplication and improve integrity. Two disciplines share the name:
DATABASE SCHEMAS
1NF: atomic values, unique rows · 2NF: no partial dependencies · 3NF: no transitive dependencies
STATISTICS / ML
Min-Max scaling to a fixed range (typically 0 to 1) · Z-score to mean 0, standard deviation 1, so no feature dominates the model.
→
3 · Harmonization
Consolidating data from disparate sources and aligning it with uniform standards: directly comparable, error-free, ready for joint analysis.
MORE THAN INTEGRATION
Integration merely gathers data in one place. Harmonization resolves substantive and logical inconsistencies.
THE GOAL
All data shares the same business meaning: semantic unity.
THE MULTI-SOURCE LIFECYCLE · REPEATS PER DATA BATCH
Ingestion
Structuring, first pass: CSV, API responses, NoSQL, scrapers arrive unstructured or semi-structured and get a uniform, readable shape.
→
Transformation
Normalization: one date standard instead of DD/MM/YYYY vs YYYY-MM-DD, duplicate records removed across sources, EUR and USD converted to a uniform scale.
→
Loading
Structuring, second pass: final schema with tables, keys, and relationships; 1NF, 2NF, 3NF or a star schema for fast business reporting.
→
Harmonization
All three disciplines combined and cycled per batch: the pipeline keeps converging on one comparable, semantically unified view.
The bottom line: don't just structure your data, don't just normalize your features. Aim for harmonization, that is where the true business value lies.
