Your RAG retrieved five chunks, and the answer sounded right. Sounding right is not being right.

The fix is not a bigger embedding model. It is structure: a taxonomy to route, an ontology to reason with, a graph to walk. Payloads do the exact filtering, two ingestion processes split expensive curation from cheap instance data, and ontology-validated multi-hop paths keep the agent from drawing impossible conclusions.

The result reads a medical chart with ICD-10 and LOINC — and reads a building with the same architecture when you swap in Brick.
