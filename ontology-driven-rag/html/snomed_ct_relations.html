SNOMED CT concept model: IS-A hierarchy plus defining attributes

A concept is defined by its parent (IS-A) plus attribute-value pairs - not by its label alone. Every attribute has a fixed domain and range, which is exactly what makes machine-checked extraction possible.

Domain and range - the machine-checkable part
Each attribute has a fixed domain (which concepts may carry it) and range (which concepts are valid values). |Finding site| ranges over |body structure| and its descendants only - an extraction cannot bind it to a random node.
Why RAG cares
Constraint-based extraction: an LLM extracting "treats" or "finding site" edges can be bound to these domains and ranges - it stops inventing relations, and entity resolution stops being a cleanup project.
Polyhierarchy
IS-A can have multiple parents: one concept, several valid classifications. The IS-A tree routes; the defining attributes reason.
